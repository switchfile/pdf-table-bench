## Abstract

This study aims to examine the factors influencing the continuation intention of enterprise metaverse technology, which includes the effects of Metaverse User Experience (MUX), Trust in the Platform (TP) as a mediator, and Technophilia (TCH) as a moderator. A cross sectional research design, quantitative approach was employed. The data is gathered from 340 employees across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, and Chandigarh in India who have already worked with metaverse technologies in their work. The research model was analysed using the method of PLS-SEM and SPSS. The results show that the Innovative Behaviour and Continuance Intention are significantly influenced by MUX, and that the Trust in Platform significantly influences the Innovative Behaviour, but has no direct influence on the Continuance Intention and relation between Innovative Behaviour and Continuance Intention is positively significant, with the interaction of Technophilia. This research builds on the growing body of research on enterprise metaverse, and combines SCT with the Information Systems Continuance Model to explain the post-adoption behavior of employees in a fully immersive work environment. Keywords: Metaverse user experience; Trust in the platform; Innovative behaviour; Technophilia; Continuance intention
