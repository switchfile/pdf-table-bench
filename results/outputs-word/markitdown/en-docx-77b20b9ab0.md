* The means in the final row are calculated from the reported sums and post counts: 48.31 / 42 ≈ 1.15; 61.79 / 78
≈ 0.79; and 110.10 / 120 ≈ 0.92. In the narrative, the authors report a mean of 1.12 for F and 0.8 for NF. These
figures do not fully match simple division of the table totals. This discrepancy should be noted in any subsequent
scholarly use.
Analytical conclusion: F content accounted for 35% of posts but 44% of cumulative mER. This indicates relatively
higher engagement as measured by mER. It does not establish that users approved of the content or that it
changed their voting behavior.
