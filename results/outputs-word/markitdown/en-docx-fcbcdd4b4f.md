Notas:

•  SNR (Signal To Noise Ratio): represents the relationship between the signal and noise at a logarithm scale

(dB)

•  CCQ (Client Connection Quality): it’s a percentage value to show the bandwidth efficiency. Its average is
given  by  Tmin/Treal,  where  Tmin  is  the  data  transmission  time  that  would  be  considered  in  a  best
condition (without any bit error rate) and Treal is the transmission time in a real situation, in other words,
considering the data retransmissions and synchronized data rate

•  CPU: indicates the CPU consumption
•  These wireless information, like synchronized data rate, signal strength, SNR and CCQ were obtained from

the client side

•  Tests durations were performed for 30s (minimum duration)
