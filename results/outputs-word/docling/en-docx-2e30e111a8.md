Fig. S1. Swelling of cast 2A-0.5AV hydrogels after 24 h of immersion in 1× PBS at 37 °C, as a function of drying route and CaCl₂ pre-crosslinking, with Kaltostat® as the commercial reference. Swelling (%) = (Wt - W0)/W0 × 100 (Eq. 9). Data are mean ± SD (n = 4 per group). Different letters indicate significant differences (Welch's one-way ANOVA followed by Games-Howell post hoc test, adjusted p &lt; 0.05). Blue, lyophilized; orange, oven-dried; grey, Kaltostat®; hatched bars, CaCl₂ pre-crosslinked. Raw data: Supplementary File 2, sheet Swelling\_liquid\_drymass\_raw.
