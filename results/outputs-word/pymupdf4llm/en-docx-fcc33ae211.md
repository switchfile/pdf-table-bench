# **Supplementary Forms A–E** 

Origin of the instruments (Forms A–D). The interview guide and the three questionnaires were developed entirely by the author for this study from the research questions and the literature reviewed in the article. They are not adapted or reproduced from any third-party instrument and contain no third-party material requiring a licence or permission. 

# **Supplementary Form A: Interview Guide (Semi-Structured)** 

# **Opening** 

1. Please describe your role in accreditation processes at your institution. 

2. How long have you been involved in accreditation activities? 

# **Accreditation Preparation** 

3. What activities does your institution undertake in preparation for accreditation? 

4. Who is involved in these activities? 

5. How would you describe the purpose of accreditation preparation? 

# **Accreditation Effects** 

6. What changes, if any, have you observed as a result of accreditation? 

7. How has accreditation affected teaching and learning? 

8. How has accreditation affected institutional resources and infrastructure? 

9. How has accreditation affected internal quality assurance processes? 

# **Motivation and Engagement** 

10. Why does your institution engage with accreditation? 

11. What would happen if accreditation were not mandatory? 

12. How would you describe the attitudes of colleagues toward accreditation? 

# **Internal Quality Assurance** 

13. How would you describe the role of the Quality Assurance Unit at your institution? 

14. How are quality assurance activities integrated into daily academic life? 

15. What challenges exist in implementing internal quality assurance? 

# **Divergent Perspectives** 

16. Are there any positive aspects of accreditation that are often overlooked? 

17. Are there any negative aspects that are often overlooked? 

# **Closing** 

18. Is there anything else you would like to add?
